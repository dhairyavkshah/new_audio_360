# Privacy Policy

**New Audio 360**

**Effective Date:** January 20, 2026

## 1. Introduction

New Audio 360 ("the App," "we," "our," or "us") is a premium music player application developed by TheTeam360. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

**Our Privacy Commitment:** New Audio 360 is designed with privacy as a core principle. The App operates primarily offline, stores all user data locally on your device, and does not transmit personal information to external servers.

By downloading, installing, or using New Audio 360, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All of the following data is stored exclusively on your device and never transmitted to our servers:

| Data Type | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Music Library Information | Display your songs and albums |
| User Preferences | Remember your settings |
| Theme Selection | Apply your chosen visual theme |
| Audio Settings | Save your equalizer and sound preferences |
| Playlists | Store your custom playlists |
| Favorites | Track your favorite songs |
| Playback History | Show recently played and most played |

### 2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

New Audio 360 does not collect, store, or transmit:

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
- Device identifiers for advertising
- Location data (except temporarily for online radio discovery)
- Usage analytics or behavioral data
- Audio files or music content
- Crash reports to external servers
- Any data to our servers

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

### 3.1 Google Play Services

For purchase processing and license verification only:

- **Purchase Processing**: Handled entirely by Google Play
- **License Verification**: Confirms legitimate Play Store installation

Google's data handling is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 3.2 Online Radio Service

When using Online Radio features:

- The App fetches station listings from a public radio directory
- Your IP address may be visible to the radio service
- No personal data is transmitted

## 4. Device Permissions

| Permission | Purpose | Required |
|------------|---------|----------|
| Media Library | Access music files on your device | Yes |
| Storage | Read music from folders | Yes |
| Location | Discover local radio stations | Optional |
| Notifications | Display playback controls | Optional |
| Internet | Online radio, license check | Yes |

All permissions are requested with clear explanations. You may deny optional permissions.

---

## 5. How We Use Information

Local data stored on your device is used solely to:

- Play your music
- Display your library
- Apply your audio settings
- Remember your preferences
- Manage your playlists
- Verify your purchase for offline use

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

---

## 6. Data Security

- Sensitive data stored using encrypted storage
- User data never leaves your device
- All network requests use encrypted connections
- No cloud backup of app data

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

- Data remains until you uninstall the App or clear app data
- We have no ability to access your data
- Uninstalling the App removes all stored data
